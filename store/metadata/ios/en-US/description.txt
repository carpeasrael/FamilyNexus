FamilyNexus combines a family calendar, a messenger and a shared shopping list in one app – without your data ending up at a central provider.

CALENDAR
• Day, week, month and agenda views, filter by person
• Private events stay on your own devices, shared events are visible to the whole family
• Recurring events with exceptions, time zones, accept and decline
• Local reminders – the notification never reveals the event title

CHAT
• Family chat and direct chats between members
• Messages, replies, reactions and attachments
• Share events as cards in the chat and answer them right there
• Online status of the family (can be switched off)

SHOPPING LIST
• Shared list for the whole family, works offline
• Check off, edit, add quantities, remove done items

SECURITY AND PRIVACY
• End-to-end encrypted over the open Matrix protocol (chat, shared events, shopping list)
• Locally encrypted database, key kept in the device key store
• App lock with the device credentials, screen protection
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics
• Bring your own server – the public server is only a default
• Free software (AGPL-3.0), source code available

FAMILY
• Moderators invite (link or QR code), approve join requests and manage roles
• New devices are verified by emoji comparison or recovery key

You create your account during setup – on matrix.org, another provider or your own server. An existing Matrix account works as well.
