New in 1.0.4:
• Task lists: moderators create lists and assign them to a family member. Each list repeats daily, weekly or monthly and restarts automatically (at midnight, on Monday or on the 1st of the month, in the list's time zone).
• Only the assigned person ticks items off, optionally with a comment. Date and time are recorded in a history that is kept across every restart of the list.
• New "Tasks for you" card on "Today"; open the tasks from the menu.
• Task lists are transmitted end-to-end encrypted like events and stored encrypted on the device.
• Fixed: tampered invitation links (for example via QR code) and event replies could crash the app.
• Fixed: devices whose time zone cannot be determined are now handled correctly as UTC.
