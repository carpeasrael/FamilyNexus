New in 1.2.0:
• Invitation links without extra confirmation: whoever opens the link and creates an account or signs in is admitted to the family automatically as soon as a moderator's app is online. Each link works once and only for a limited time.
• Join without a link: moderators share the family code (Family → Invite someone), the person joining enters it under "Join". A moderator confirms explicitly.
• Declined join requests are now shown. Open requests appear as a badge on the Family tab.
• In the invite dialog, "Advanced" lets you choose the server for new accounts.
• If a server does not allow sign-up in the app, Element can be started.
Important: please update all moderators. Older versions do not recognise the new invitation links and do not confirm them automatically.
