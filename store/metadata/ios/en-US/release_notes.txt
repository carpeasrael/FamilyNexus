New in 1.1.2:
• Invitation links now name the server for your account and sign-in. The app uses exactly that server, even if you already have an account. A server other than matrix.org appears in the field "Use another provider".
• After sign-in and the emergency key, the family from the link appears; one tap sends the join request.
Important: new invitation links can only be read by devices with this version. Please update all devices in your family.
