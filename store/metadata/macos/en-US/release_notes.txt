New in 1.2.3:
• Security: The identity records of family members are also kept encrypted on the device; if the server changes a key, it is noticed.
• The loading screen at startup follows the device's appearance.
• Calendar: shows how far the year is (day, days left, percent); the calendar also syncs with the server every 15 minutes.
• New users get a short introduction to calendar, chat and shopping; it can be watched again in the settings.
• Settings: “Share app” copies the link to the store listing, “Rate app” opens it (Android).

New in 1.2.2:
• Please update all devices of your family. Older app versions do not show groups, items with combined emojis or new reactions; if someone edits or checks off an item there, its group is lost for everyone.
• Shopping: items can be assigned to a group, for example a shop. The list shows open items by group, “No group” at the end.
• Today: at most three events with “All events”; the “Reminders” card opens an overview where due reminders can be confirmed.
• A moderator now confirms every join via invitation link. Invitation links no longer show names and server in readable form and no longer contain the family name; still treat the link like a key. Older app versions cannot read new links: please update all moderators.
• Settings → Account → “Change password”. New accounts ask for the password twice.
• Security: reactions are sent encrypted; unencrypted events in the family room are no longer trusted; after switching families no data of the old family reaches the new one; servers in the home network are only used with consent, also when signing in via the browser.
• Reliability: an error while restoring the sign-in no longer deletes family data; failed messages are resent with a pause; loaded images stay with their message; canceled saving no longer reports “Saved”.
• Times follow the device's 12/24-hour setting everywhere. Combined emojis such as 👨‍👩‍👧 are allowed in shopping and tasks, still not in events; input fields and chat count emojis correctly.
• The privacy policy in the app names key storage, permissions and backups matching the platform.
• Further fixes and speed-ups, among others when loading the calendar, sending photos and syncing.
• Mac: the app states the AGPL license instead of “All rights reserved”.
