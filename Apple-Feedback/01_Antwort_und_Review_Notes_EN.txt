2. Purpose and audience
FamilyNexus combines appointments, household tasks, shopping and encrypted family/direct chat to reduce scattered messages and missed arrangements. Adults organize families; minors participate with guardian consent and moderator invitation. It is a consumer app, not limited to specific businesses/employees.

3. Setup and access
Setup/sync require internet and a Matrix account. Both review accounts use matrix.org and the same sample family:
R1 moderator: username/password in Sign-in information; emergency key [R1_KEY].
R2 member: username [R2_USER]; password [R2_PASSWORD]; emergency key [R2_KEY].
Launch > "I already have an account" > "Sign in at matrix.org" > browser login > return > "Unlock this device" > account's emergency key > "Unlock". Sign out of other browser accounts first.
Today: upcoming items. Calendar > "New event": title/date, shared/private, save. Chat: send a message; Family > member > "Send message" opens direct chat. Shopping: add/check off items. Top-right menu > Tasks: moderators create/assign lists; assignees complete items. Family: moderator invitations/member management. Sample events, chats, shopping items and tasks are provided. No sample files required; chat attachments are optional. Reminders need notification permission; chat has no push notifications.
Register: "Start a new family" > name > "Create account at matrix.org" > provider steps > return > "Create emergency key" > store/confirm > "Next" > family name > "Create family".
Delete: menu > Settings > Account > "Delete account" > "Open deletion page" > confirm with provider > return > "Check deletion" if needed. Local data is cleared; messages received by others may remain. Use a disposable account.
Report: long-press a chat message > "Report message" > reason > "Report". Server receives identifiers/reason/optional text, not decrypted content. "Block sender" hides messages/direct chat on this device. Undo: Settings > "Blocked members". Moderators can remove members.

4. Services and tools
Matrix homeserver (default matrix.org): accounts, encrypted sync/media, reports; account.matrix.org: browser registration/login/deletion, possibly email/CAPTCHA. Alternative HTTPS providers: Advanced. GitHub Pages: invitation page/OAuth client website. Element: optional registration fallback. Local tools: Flutter, Matrix Dart SDK, vodozemac, SQLCipher; Apple Keychain, device authentication, local notifications. No ad, analytics, AI, payment, external content-data or remote push service integrated.
